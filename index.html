How the HighLevel integration works

You don't leave HighLevel and you don't rebuild anything. Commas connects to your HighLevel account with an API key, and from then on payments, buyers and financing approvals flow both ways.

  1. 1

    Grab your HighLevel access token

    In HighLevel, create a private integration under Settings → Private Integrations and copy the token. Takes two minutes, and your account manager can do it with you on a screen share.

  2. 2

    Paste it into Commas

    In Commas, go to Settings → Integrations → CRM and connect HighLevel. Your Commas account is now linked to your sub-account.

  3. 3

    Keep selling the way you do now

    Send the same invoices, funnels and booking links. Commas handles the checkout, and each payment lands back on the contact in HighLevel so your pipeline and automations keep firing.

Commas isn't listed in HighLevel's app marketplace search. It connects through the private integration above. Commas also has an open API and an MCP server if you want to go further.

Questions photographers ask

Before you book the call

Is there really no monthly fee?

Yes. Commas costs $0 a month, with no setup fee and no contract. It only earns a flat rate when you process a payment.

Do I have to leave HighLevel or rebuild my funnels?

No. Commas connects to your HighLevel account through a private integration, so your funnels, calendars, invoices and workflows stay where they are. Your account manager sets it up with you.

Can I host my community on Commas?

Yes. Channels, a course library, live Q&A and paid VIP rooms are built in, and memberships bill through the same checkout. You can drop the separate community subscription.

How does the 20% referral work?

Introduce someone to Luis. Once they're processing on Commas, you earn 20% of Commas' net margin on their account every month for as long as they stay. Luis attributes them to you and you can track it in your dashboard.

I'm outside the US. Does this work for me?

Financing is US-only for now. Card processing options vary by country, so text Luis and he'll tell you what's available where you are.
